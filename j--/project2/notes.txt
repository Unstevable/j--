1. Provide a high-level description (ie, using minimal amount of technical
   jargon) of the project in no more than 200 words.

This project revolves around modifying Scanner.java and TokenInfo.java to recognize various operations, reserved words, multi-line comments, long and double literals.  The various operations that were added included:  ?, :, ~, !=, /, /=, -=, *=, %, %=, >>, >>=, >>>, >>>=, >=, <<, <<=, <, ^, ^=, |, |=, ||, & and &=, with some overlap from operations already implemented in project one.  The reserved words now recognized by the Scanner are:  break, case, catch, continue, default, do, double, finally, for, implements, interface, long, switch, throw, throws and try.  The slightly harder aspects of this project were to add multi-line comment functionality to the Scanner, and adding double and long literals to the scanner.  The multi-line comment required creating a DFA beforehand to properly visualize where there should be conditions that would create a multi-line comment and what conditions would not.  The long literals were easy, as simple as adding a check for ‘l’ or ‘L’ after searching for digits.  However, double literals were much more tedious to implement; it required being aware of whether or not a ‘.’ was present, an ‘e’ or ‘E’, a ‘d’ or ‘D’, and so on.
